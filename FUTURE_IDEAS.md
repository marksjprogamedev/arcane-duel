# Arcane Duel — Future Ideas Backlog

This file is for ideas to consider **later**. Nothing listed here is approved for implementation. Keep the playable game unchanged until an idea is explicitly selected.

## Status key
- **Idea** — proposed, not yet approved
- **Discuss** — needs design decisions
- **Approved** — user has explicitly chosen to implement
- **Done** — implemented and verified

## Spell ideas

| Idea | Concept | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Whirlpool (Water) | Summon a swirling pool that pulls opponents toward its centre and deals repeated damage. | Idea |
| Hydroblade (Water) | Launch a fast, crescent-shaped blade of water that cuts through opponents. | Idea |
| Bubble Prison (Water) | Temporarily trap an opponent in a floating bubble; damage breaks the bubble. | Idea |
| Geyser (Water) | Trigger a column of water beneath a targeted area, launching opponents upward. | Idea |
| Riptide (Water) | Send a rushing ground current that drags opponents in its direction. | Idea |
| Water Whip (Water) | Lash a flexible stream toward the cursor to damage and briefly slow opponents. | Idea |

These Water concepts were discussed as possible replacements after removing Tidal Surge. **No replacement has been chosen.**

## How to add ideas

For each idea, record:
1. **Name and category**
2. **What it does**
3. **Why it might improve the game**
4. **Open questions or possible drawbacks**
5. **Status**

**Rule:** Recording an idea in this file does not change game code or mean it should be implemented.
