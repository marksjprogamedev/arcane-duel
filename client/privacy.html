Privacy

Arcane Duel is a small hobby game. You can play without an account. If you make one, this is everything we keep and why.

What we store

  • Your username (how you log in and how friends find you).
  • Your display name (shown to other players in rooms).
  • Your password and recovery code, only as scrambled hashes: we can't read them, only check them.
  • Later, when those features arrive: your saved wizard look, spell loadouts and friends list.
  • A login cookie, so you stay logged in on this browser.

What we don't

No email, no real name, no age or birthday, no IP addresses kept with your account, no tracking, no ads, no analytics.

Why

Only to run the game: to log you in, show your name to the players you duel, and keep your settings between visits.

Who sees it

Nobody else. Nothing is shared or sold. Other players see only your display name and username.

Deleting your account

Click your name in the top-right corner of the main menu, then Delete. Your account and everything saved with it are gone straight away. Encrypted nightly backups age out within 30 days.
