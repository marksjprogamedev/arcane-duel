# Notes for AI assistants

- **Asked to test the game, or to help someone test it?** Open `docs/TEST-CHECKLIST.md`. It tells you
  how to walk the tester through the checks in order, how to tick them off, and where to record
  results. Do that instead of improvising a test plan.
- **Working on the code?** Read `docs/HANDOFF.md` first, then `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`. Every feature
  adds its manual test items to `docs/TEST-CHECKLIST.md`, bumps the version and adds a
  `CHANGELOG.md` entry (rules at the top of that file).
